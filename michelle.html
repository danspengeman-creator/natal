Taurus — The Full Portrait

Earth · Fixed · VenusElement, Modality and Ruler

Where other signs seek experience or meaning or power, Taurus seeks quality. In everything.

Taurus is the most patient and the most stubborn sign in the zodiac — and these are the same quality wearing different hats. Michelle's Aries Moon adds a fieriness and directness that keeps the Taurus steadiness from tipping into inertia.
