Name Meaning: Maybelle June

First name

Maybelle

"Lovable" and "beautiful"

Origin: English and French

Maybelle is a blend of May, the spring month, and belle, the French word for beautiful. It also works as a spelling variant of Mabel, which comes from the Latin amabilis, meaning lovable. The name was most popular in the late 1800s and has been rediscovered by modern parents who like vintage names.

Middle name

June

"Of Juno" or "young"

Origin: Latin

From the Latin Junius, the month named for Juno, the Roman goddess of marriage and family. An older reading ties the month to iuniores, the young ones. As a given name, June was especially popular in the early 20th century.

A spring-and-summer name set: a lovable, beautiful May followed by a June, the month of beginnings.

Why we chose it

The family wanted an M name for Maybelle. Michelle also likes the double L in the name, and Dan came across Maybelle as the pick. She was expected around May or June, so her full name, May-belle June, holds both of those months. It can be said Maybell or Maybelle, and the family happily accepts either version from anyone who says her name.

Natal Chart Wheel

The chart wheel is the traditional circular diagram astrologers use to show a complete birth chart at a glance. The outer ring marks the twelve zodiac signs at their fixed positions in the sky. The inner spokes mark the twelve houses, drawn here as equal houses starting from the Ascendant, shown in gold where the four angular houses begin. The gold dashed line marks the true Midheaven, calculated directly rather than approximated. The faint lines crossing the center connect planets in a meaningful aspect to one another, teal for trine, blue for sextile, rose for square or opposition. Hover or tap a planet for its exact degree and house, or a house wedge for what that area of life covers.

Generators are the most common type, roughly 37% of humanity, and are the literal life-force of the planet. The Generator aura is open and enveloping, drawing life toward it.
