Generators are the most common type — roughly 37% of humanity — and are the literal life-force of the planet. The Generator aura is open and enveloping, drawing life toward it. The not-self theme — the signal that something is off — is frustration, which arises when a Generator initiates rather than responds, or commits to things that do not truly excite them.
